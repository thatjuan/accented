⌘ ⌘

The accent picker you can configure.

Hold-to-accent is slow, it vanishes when key repeat is on, and it dumps every mark for every language you enabled. Accented is a double-press of ⌘ and a small strip of only the characters you use.

Download for macOS ↓ v0.4.0

macOS 13 or later. Menu bar app. Free.

  1. 01

    Double-press ⌘.

    The picker appears wherever you are typing. ⌥Space still works if you prefer a combo.

  2. 02

    Pick from your set.

    If you just typed a, you see á (and only what you enabled for a). If you did not, you see your whole set in one row.

  3. 03

    It lands in the field.

    Numbers, arrows, or a click. The picker goes away. The character replaces or inserts.

Languages

Turn on what you write. Leave the rest off.

Spanish without the French extras. Two languages if that's how you write. The picker is the union of every language you enable, and you can switch off individual characters. Or build a named palette of just the characters you want, say á í ñ, and enable it like a language.

01 — Trigger

Instant, not a hold.

No press-and-hold delay. Works with key repeat on. Same trigger in every app.

02 — Languages

Yours, not everyone else's.

Spanish, French, Portuguese, German, Italian, Catalan, Turkish. On, off, pick characters one by one, or build your own named palette.

03 — Native

A real Mac app.

Menu bar, no Dock icon, updates itself, Accessibility asked once and explained.

Only the marks you actually use.
