The accent picker macOS almost shipped.

Hold-to-accent is slow, it vanishes when key repeat is on, and it dumps every mark for every language you enabled. Accented is a global hotkey and a small strip of only the characters you use.

Download for macOS ↓ v0.1.0

macOS 13 or later. Menu bar app. Free.

  1. 01

    Type, then trigger.

    Keep writing. When you need a mark, press ⌥Space (or your own combo).

  2. 02

    Pick from your set.

    If you just typed a, you see á (and only what you enabled for a). If you did not, you see your whole set in one row.

  3. 03

    It lands in the field.

    Numbers, arrows, or a click. The picker goes away. The character replaces or inserts.
